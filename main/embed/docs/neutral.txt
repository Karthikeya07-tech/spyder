Twist neutral pose (measured on the robot)
===========================================

The reference start and stand pose. Every walk and turn starts here and eases back here.
Measured on the real robot on 2026-11-02 after the microcontroller's `neutral` action.
Source: hardware/neutral_pose.yaml

Servos: 12 x AX-12A. ticks 0..1023 = 0..300 deg, 512 = 150 deg = middle.
degrees = ticks * 300 / 1023
Legs: leg1 = rear-right, leg2 = front-right, leg3 = front-left, leg4 = rear-left.
Per leg: hip_yaw, hip_pitch, knee.

In Gazebo this pose is the spawn pose (every joint 0 rad).
